# Alleviant Medical

> Alleviant Medical, Inc. is a clinical-stage medical device company in Austin, Texas, founded
> in 2017, developing minimally invasive transcatheter therapies for heart failure. The
> Alleviant System is an investigational, no-implant approach that creates a small interatrial
> passageway to relieve elevated left atrial pressure without leaving a permanent device
> behind. It holds U.S. FDA Breakthrough Device designation for both preserved (HFpEF) and
> reduced (HFrEF) ejection fraction heart failure and is under study in the FDA-approved
> ALLAY-HF and ALLAY-HFrEF pivotal trials. The Alleviant System is investigational only and is
> not cleared, authorized or approved for commercial use in any geography.
